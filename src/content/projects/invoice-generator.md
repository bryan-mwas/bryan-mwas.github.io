## The problem
Small business owners, independent contractors, and freelancers often waste valuable time manually formatting invoices in spreadsheets or wrestling with complicated, paid accounting software just to bill a customer or submit a quote.

## What I built
A streamlined, fast invoice and quotation builder created with Next.js and TypeScript. It offers an intuitive interface where users can customize company branding, input line items, apply tax rates, calculate discounts, and export clean, production-grade PDF invoices ready for delivery to clients.

## Key features
- **Instant Quotation & Invoicing**: Dynamically generates itemized subtotals, custom tax calculations, and currency formats.
- **Client-Side Generation**: Works smoothly with zero server friction, ensuring immediate feedback and fast export.
- **Print & PDF Ready**: Produces cleanly styled, printable layouts suitable for both digital sharing and physical archiving.
