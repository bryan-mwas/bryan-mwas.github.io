## The problem
Borrowers and financial planners frequently struggle to understand how extra payments, varying interest rates, or different loan tenures affect their debt payoff timeline. Most online loan calculators only show a flat monthly figure without demonstrating how custom repayments compress loan duration and save interest over time.

## What I built
An interactive, client-side loan amortization scenario simulator. Users can enter a principal amount, annual interest rate, and term in years, and then simulate custom monthly EMI contributions. The tool computes monthly interest splits, principal deductions, running balances, and exact debt-free completion dates in real time.

## Key features
- **Custom EMI Simulation**: Compare standard contractual installments against custom monthly payments to view accelerated payoff dates.
- **Scenario Comparison**: Generate and compare multiple loan structures side-by-side to determine optimal repayment strategies.
- **Detailed Amortization Schedules**: Expandable breakdowns displaying opening balance, monthly EMI, interest portion, principal portion, and cumulative interest paid.
